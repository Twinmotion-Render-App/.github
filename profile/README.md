## 🚀 Introduction
So, what is Twinmotion? It is a standalone real-time rendering suite for Windows that takes the geometry from your design tools and lets you dress it with materials, lighting, and life, all while previewing the result instantly. Rather than waiting on lengthy render jobs, you sculpt the scene live and decide exactly how each Twinmotion render should look. It suits architects, interior designers, and landscape professionals who want to communicate ideas clearly and quickly.

## ✨ Highlights

### Design and Explore
- **Instant feedback** — Every material, light, and object updates live, so the viewport always reflects your latest decision.
- **Direct model links** — Connect to CAD and BIM software and keep imported geometry synchronized as your source model evolves.
- **Deep asset library** — Populate scenes with plants, people, vehicles, and surfaces using simple drag-and-drop.

### Present and Share
- **Cinematic output** — Compose a Twinmotion render as a high-resolution still, a 360° panorama, or a walkthrough video.
- **Atmosphere control** — Shift the sun, seasons, and weather to present a design under any lighting condition.

## 💻 System Specs
**Minimum**
- OS: Windows 10 64-bit
- Processor: A modern multi-core 64-bit processor
- Memory: 16 GB RAM
- Graphics: A dedicated GPU with current drivers
- Storage: Several gigabytes of free space for the app and assets

**Recommended**
- OS: Windows 11 64-bit
- Processor: A recent high-performance multi-core processor
- Memory: 32 GB RAM or more
- Graphics: A high-end dedicated GPU with plenty of video memory
- Storage: A fast SSD with generous free space for large projects

## 🎯 Getting Up and Running
1. Import a model or establish a direct link from your design application.
2. Apply materials and drop in landscaping, furniture, and characters.
3. Fine-tune the lighting, weather, and time of day.
4. Walk through the scene in real time to choose your best angles.
5. Export the finished Twinmotion render as an image, panorama, or animation.

## ❓ Common Questions
**Is Twinmotion free?**
A free edition and a free trial have been available so you can evaluate the software without paying, while advanced or commercial use may require a paid license. Confirm the latest details on the official Twinmotion website.

**Which versions of Windows are supported?**
Twinmotion works on modern 64-bit Windows, including Windows 10 and Windows 11, and a dedicated graphics card is recommended for smooth real-time rendering.

**Is my data secure?**
Your scenes and assets are saved locally on your Windows PC, so your files remain under your control. Keep regular backups and secure your account to safeguard your work.
